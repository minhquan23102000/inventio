# Incident 2026-03-14: no backup to restore

## What happened

The `nightly_backup` run filled the storage bucket at 03:40 and stopped without an error, and
nobody checked the scheduler. At 11:00 a bad migration corrupted the orders table; the newest
usable backup was two days old. 1,870 orders placed in between were rebuilt by hand from
payment receipts over three days.

## What changed

The 07:00 scheduler check was added to the runbook, and the job now pages the on-call engineer
when it has not finished by 06:30 or when the bucket is more than 90% full.
