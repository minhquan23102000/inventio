When the nightly backup has not finished

  1. 101. Check the scheduler at 07:00. If `nightly_backup` is still running or has failed, stop it.
  2. 112. Take a fresh backup from the replica, not the primary: `make backup SOURCE=replica`.
  3. 123. Tell the support desk that order exports may be slow until it finishes.
